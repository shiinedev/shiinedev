Conversion work gets a bad reputation because it is usually reduced to bigger buttons and louder colours. That version of the job is easy to dismiss, and it deserves to be.

## Editing, not decorating

The work that actually moves numbers is closer to editing than decoration. You remove the sentence that makes someone hesitate, you move the proof next to the claim it supports, and you cut the third option nobody picks.

Craft and conversion only conflict when craft is confused with ornament. A page that loads fast, reads cleanly, and answers the next question at the moment it forms is both the better-designed page and the better-performing one.

## The working rule

Every element earns its place by answering a question a buyer is already asking. If it does not, it goes — regardless of how nice it looks in the mockup.
